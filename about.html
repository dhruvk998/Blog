About Me

Hi! My name is Dhruv Kaushik, an Engineering Physics senior @ IITM, India. I began this site recently as an attempt to write about fairly technical ideas, catered to an audience who may have some background in those topics. To satisfy both the reader wanting to quickly scan through, as well as the reader who wants to understand stuff exactly, I have seeked to make the reading a fully customizable experience. Though I try keeping the tone fairly simple, every non-trivial claim I may gloss over is explained inline. Simply click the the theorem text▸ to expand them.
